## What it is
This is a 3D modelled enclosure for the MacroPad PCB project I designed in week 1. My MacroPad will be used to provide common shortcuts for apps I use, especially creative ones like music creation or video editing software. 

## How I made it
I modelled this in Onshape. It was my first time ever using Onshape, so it took me quite a while to get used to it and work out how to model properly. Being able to import a 3D model of the PCB was really helpful, though, as it made getting the dimensions right much easier.

The enclosure is made of two parts - a base, which the PCB will attach to with threaded inserts and M3 screws, and a lid, which will magnetically snap on top of the box (in case I need to fix anything). The box has 2.5mm thick walls, with a 10mm thick lip at the top, deep enough for the magnets to be countersunk into it. There is 8mm between the bottom of the PCB and the base of the enclosure, to allow components to poke through underneath (they are all, after all, through-hole components). The threaded inserts will be... well... inserted into some pillars at the bottom of the box. 

Because I designed the box to be 10mm wider and 10mm taller than the actual PCB, I can have a 10mm lip on the box and edge on the lid. This creates the illusion that the PCB is bigger and had more space left around the components, when in fact, there is barely any room around the edges (to keep costs down). This 10mm lip also allows the magnets to be inserted in the lid. They will be fixed in place with superglue, and hopefully create a nice strong bond that can still be broken easily enough by sliding the lid a bit to pull the magnets apart (pulling straight up might pull the magnets out their holes). 

## Design language
Overall, I've gone for a fairly basic, modern look. Rounded corners on the lid, right angles on the edges of the container, and nothing too tall or bulky. I will have it printed by JLC3DP in black resin (for the lid, as they won't print PLA under 10mm thick, and the lid is 5mm thick) and black PLA (for the base).
